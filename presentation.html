The Real Problem

Vendor Payment Fraud & The Verification Bottleneck

⚠️ The Anatomy of a BEC / Invoice Attack

Fraudsters compromise legitimate vendor emails or spoof domains, submit authentic-looking GST invoices, and request urgent disbursement to a new unverified mule bank account / IFSC.

1. Compromised Supplier Email
2. Authentic-Looking ₹48.2 Lakh Invoice
3. Bank Switch: Chase/HDFC ➔ Offshore/Mule Bank
4. Finance Approves ➔ 💸 Money Gone Forever

🛑 Why Current Solutions Fail

Traditional OCR / ERPs: Matches PO numbers, but blind to subtle bank account / IFSC mutations.
Static Rule Engines: Floods teams with false alarms on normal business surges.
Manual Human Calling: Unscalable for thousands of monthly invoices; causes massive delays.
